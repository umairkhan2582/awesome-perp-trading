# Awesome Perpetual Futures Trading [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of perpetual futures exchanges, tools, analytics, and resources for traders and developers.

Perpetual futures (perps) are the most traded instrument in crypto, with daily volume exceeding $100B across centralized and decentralized exchanges. This list aims to be the most comprehensive resource for anyone trading, building, or researching perps.

## Decentralized Exchanges (DEX)

### Orderbook-Based

- [Hyperliquid](https://hyperliquid.xyz) — High-performance L1 with fully onchain orderbook. Largest perp DEX by volume.
- [dYdX](https://dydx.trade) — Cosmos-based orderbook DEX with decentralized governance.
- [Aevo](https://aevo.xyz) — Options and perpetuals with off-chain orderbook, on-chain settlement.
- [Backpack](https://backpack.exchange) — Regulated exchange built on Solana with perp trading.
- [Lighter](https://lighter.xyz) — Zero-gas orderbook DEX on Arbitrum.
- [Grvt](https://grvt.io) — Hybrid DEX with off-chain matching and on-chain settlement on zkSync.
- [edgeX](https://edgex.exchange) — High-performance DEX with CEX-like trading experience.
- [Paradex](https://paradex.trade) — Starknet-based perp DEX with self-custody.
- [RabbitX](https://rabbitx.io) — Starknet orderbook DEX with fast execution.
- [LogX](https://logx.trade) — Multi-chain orderbook aggregator for perps.

### AMM / Pool-Based

- [GMX](https://gmx.io) — Decentralized spot and perp exchange on Arbitrum and Avalanche. Pioneered GLP liquidity model.
- [Jupiter Perps](https://jup.ag/perps) — Perp trading on Solana via Jupiter's JLP pool.
- [Gains Network (gTrade)](https://gains.trade) — Synthetic leverage trading with gDAI vault.
- [Synthetix Perps](https://synthetix.io) — Synthetic perps powering multiple frontends (Kwenta, Polynomial).
- [MUX Protocol](https://mux.network) — Leveraged trading aggregator across multiple DEXes.
- [Vertex Protocol](https://vertexprotocol.com) — Hybrid orderbook-AMM DEX on Arbitrum.
- [HMX](https://hmx.org) — Cross-margin perps on Arbitrum with multi-asset collateral.
- [Drift Protocol](https://drift.trade) — Full-featured DEX on Solana with perps, spot, and lending.
- [Perpetual Protocol](https://perp.com) — Virtual AMM (vAMM) pioneer on Optimism.
- [ApolloX](https://apollox.finance) — DEX with multi-chain support and copy trading.
- [Kiloex](https://kiloex.io) — Perp DEX on opBNB and Manta with price oracle model.
- [SynFutures](https://synfutures.com) — Permissionless perp DEX with Oyster AMM.

## Centralized Exchanges (CEX)

- [Binance Futures](https://www.binance.com/en/futures) — Largest CEX by perp volume. USDM and COINM contracts.
- [Bybit](https://www.bybit.com) — Major derivatives exchange with unified trading account.
- [OKX](https://www.okx.com) — Top-tier exchange with perps, options, and DeFi integration.
- [Bitget](https://www.bitget.com) — Derivatives-focused CEX with copy trading.
- [Gate.io](https://www.gate.io) — Wide asset selection with perp futures.
- [dYdX (Pro)](https://dydx.exchange) — Centralized matching with decentralized settlement.
- [MEXC](https://www.mexc.com) — Zero-fee spot trading, competitive perp fees.
- [HTX (Huobi)](https://www.htx.com) — Established exchange with linear and inverse perps.
- [BingX](https://www.bingx.com) — Social trading platform with perps.
- [Phemex](https://www.phemex.com) — Derivatives exchange with contract and spot trading.

## Analytics & Data

- [PerpFinder](https://perpfinder.com) — Compare perp DEXes by volume, fees, open interest, TVL, and funding rates. Side-by-side exchange comparisons.
- [DefiLlama Derivatives](https://defillama.com/derivatives) — DEX derivatives volume and TVL tracking.
- [CoinGlass](https://coinglass.com) — Futures data: open interest, liquidations, funding rates, long/short ratios.
- [Coinalyze](https://coinalyze.net) — Advanced futures analytics with aggregated OI, funding, and liquidation data.
- [Laevitas](https://laevitas.ch) — Derivatives analytics covering perps, options, and volatility.
- [Velo Data](https://velo.xyz) — Institutional-grade derivatives data and analytics.
- [The Block Data](https://www.theblock.co/data) — Crypto market data including derivatives metrics.
- [Token Terminal](https://tokenterminal.com) — Financial metrics for DeFi protocols including perp DEXes.
- [Dune Analytics](https://dune.com) — Community-built dashboards for on-chain perp data.
